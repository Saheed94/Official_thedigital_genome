Refund Policy

The Digital Genome (TDG) understands that circumstances may change. Refund requests are handled according to the following terms:

Before training begins

Students who request cancellation before the first training session will be eligible for a full refund if the request is made within seven (7) calendar days of payment. If the request is made more than seven (7) calendar days after payment, a 10% administrative fee will be deducted from the amount paid, together with any applicable non-recoverable transaction charges.

After training begins

Refunds are generally not available once the student has attended the first training session or accessed substantial training materials, except where required by applicable law or approved by TDG under exceptional circumstances.

Cancellation by TDG

If TDG cancels the programme and cannot offer a suitable alternative, the student will receive a full refund of the training fee paid.

How to request a refund

All refund requests must be submitted in writing to support@thedigitalgenome.com. The request must include the student's full name, programme enrolled in, and reason for the request.

Refund processing time

Approved refunds will be processed within five (5) working days of approval. The time required for the funds to appear in the student's account may depend on the relevant banking institution.

Applicable rights

This policy does not limit any consumer rights or refund entitlements that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Submitting a registration form does not guarantee enrolment. This policy applies to students who have paid for the programme.
